## What changed since the 20261123 record, and why

The 20261123 record's caveat was that `sg13g2_inv_2`/`inv_4` deltas were
confounded: the schematic NMOS stated `ng=1`/`ng=2` while the layout draws
2/4 fingers (the layout folds every device on a gate net to that gate's
largest `ng`, because one poly column crosses both pull networks). The fold
now lives in `design/gen_netlists.py` (`Cell.reconcile_gate_fingers`, commit
67f6c15): the schematic states the drawn decomposition -- `inv_2` NMOS
`w=1000n ng=2`, `inv_4` NMOS `w=2000n ng=4`, total width unchanged. LVS
re-run on all four cells: `match`. `inv_1` and `nand2_1` netlists are
byte-identical to before, so they are the controls.

`model_mismatch` stays `count_only` on `inv_2`/`inv_4` (one `ng=2`/`ng=4`
device line vs 2/4 extracted finger instances) -- an instance-counting
artifact of `ng=` vs per-finger instances, not a geometry disagreement.

## Reading the rows

- **The representation confound is measured now.** With the schematic stating
  the drawn fingers, `inv_2`'s and `inv_4`'s worst failing deltas drop
  (9.18 -> 5.87 %, 18.90 -> 13.66 %) and the NMOS/PMOS asymmetry of the 20261123
  record is gone as a diagnostic signal: what remains is parasitics plus the
  Liberty disagreement below.
- **The as-drawn cells disagree with the committed Liberty view.** 44
  (`inv_2`) and 45 (`inv_4`) rows now fail **pre-layout**, on the schematic
  side alone: the Liberty view was characterized (item 5, #180) from the
  `ng=1`/`ng=2` decomposition that the layout never drew. The PDK's
  `sg13_lv_nmos` subckt computes junction AS/AD/PS/PD from `w` and `ng`
  (`sg13g2_moslv_mod.lib`): the even-`ng` branches give the folded device a
  larger PS/PD than the single-finger estimate the Liberty run used, so the
  as-drawn cell is slower on the affected edges. No layout or tolerance change
  can fix that: the reference view itself describes an undrawn device. The
  options are re-characterizing Liberty from the reconciled netlists or
  re-fingering the cells (a `finger_max_p` change that redraws every PMOS) --
  both are spec-level decisions, out of scope here, filed as follow-ups.
- **`inv_1` (control) reproduces the 20261123 run** (25 rows, +2.08 .. +6.08 %
  at 1-3 fF loads): the ~0.2 fF extracted on Y (C_Y 202.6 aF, R_Y 2 x 0.77
  ohm) against the 1-3 fF bench load. This is the parasitic floor of the
  drawn output node; rows at larger loads sit at the 0.05-0.4 % floor.
- **`inv_2`/`inv_4` parasitics** (same extraction as 20261123, deck at
  0.9.3.dev4954): `inv_2` Y 4 x 0.385 ohm, C_Y 202.6 aF, VSS 4 x 0.496 ohm,
  VDD 4 x 0.622 ohm; `inv_4` Y 8 x 0.41-0.48 ohm, C_Y 450.1 aF, VSS
  8 x 0.17-0.50 ohm, C_VSS 394.5 aF, VDD 8 x 0.23-0.70 ohm, C_VDD 473.0 aF.
  `inv_4`'s output C doubles against `inv_2` on the same load grid, which is
  why its failing rows extend to +13.7 %.
- **`sg13g2_nand2_1`'s failing rows are negative** (-2.0 .. -5.3 %, 1-3 fF
  loads): the extracted side is *faster* than the Liberty window. Its
  extraction (stack node `$2` 2 x 0.289 ohm, C 83.9 aF; Y 3 x 0.47-0.91 ohm,
  C_Y 245.8 aF) also passes explicit per-finger AS/AD from the drawn
  geometry, which for the wide single fingers (W=1U) is smaller than the
  model's internal z1/z2-based estimate the schematic leg uses
  (`sg13g2_moslv_mod.lib`: "if as is given externally, no adjustment for ng
  is done"), so the extracted junction load is lighter at the same net C.
  The sign flip is an extraction-vs-model geometry-accounting difference at
  light load, recorded as a finding.
